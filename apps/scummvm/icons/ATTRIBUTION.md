# Icon artwork attribution

`source/agi.svg` and `source/sci.svg` are engine icons from the ScummVM Icons
project: https://github.com/scummvm/scummvm-icons

The project distributes its artwork under GPL-2.0 (see its `LICENSE`). The
engine icons in `agi/icon.png` and `sci/icon.png` are rasterized adaptations
with the existing ScummVM wordmark from `apps/scummvm/icon.png` composited on
top. The original SVGs are retained in `source/` for attribution and
reproducibility.
